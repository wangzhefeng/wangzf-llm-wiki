**Welcome to Gurobi Optimizer! We're here to help you get started!**

## Basic steps to get started with Gurobi Optimizer

- First, [obtain a Gurobi license](https://support.gurobi.com/hc/en-us/articles/12684663118993).
- Next, install [Gurobi Optimizer](https://support.gurobi.com/hc/en-us/articles/4534161999889) or [Gurobi Compute Server](https://support.gurobi.com/hc/en-us/articles/20167777205777).
- Then, [retrieve and set up your license](https://support.gurobi.com/hc/en-us/articles/12872879801105).
- Finally, [test your installation and license](https://support.gurobi.com/hc/en-us/articles/13417565229713).
	- Also see [How do I use the Gurobi setup diagnostic tool?](https://support.gurobi.com/hc/en-us/articles/39656220420241)
