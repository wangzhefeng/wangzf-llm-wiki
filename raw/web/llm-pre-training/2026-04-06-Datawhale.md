Datawhale 是一个专注于AI领域的开源组织，成立于2018年，我们汇聚了一群有开源精神和探索精神的理想主义者，致力于分享最前沿的AI知识，改善学习环境，我们的价值观是：for the learner，和学习者一起成长。

Datawhale is an AI-focused open source organization founded in 2018. We bring together a group of idealists with an open-source ethos and a spirit of exploration, committed to sharing cutting-edge AI knowledge and improving the learning environment. Our core value is: for the learner, growing alongside learners.
