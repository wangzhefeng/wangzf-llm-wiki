This is the Cartography Playground, a simple and interactive website for explaining cartographic algorithms, problems and other matters.  
It is aimed at students of cartography who want to refresh and deepen their knowledge.

Cartography Playground is build as part of bachelor's and master's theses at the [Chair of Cartography and Visual Analytics](https://www.asg.ed.tum.de/lfk/home/) at the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.tum.de/en).

Please use an up to date, modern browser like Firefox or Chrome with JavaScript enabled and SVG support to make sure all playgrounds work as intended.
